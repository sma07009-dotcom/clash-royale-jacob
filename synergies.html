Synergies

5 synergies2-4 cards eachUse cases

Evo Royal Giant + Fisherman

2 cards
Evo Royal GiantFisherman

The pull support drags buildings, tanks, or mini-tanks out of the win condition's path so it gets extra tower shots.

Best use cases

Use it when the opponent depends on a central building, ground tank, or melee defender.

Pros and cons

  • Simple two-card pressure
  • Great at breaking building defenses
  • Needs careful pull placement

Hog Rider + Firecracker Evolution

2 cards
Hog RiderFirecracker Evolution

Bridge pressure forces a quick defensive answer while ranged splash support chips from far away.

Best use cases

Best when the opponent's spell is out of cycle or their building gives the ranged support a tower-splash line.

Pros and cons

  • Very easy pressure pattern
  • Forces awkward spell choices
  • A pull spell can ruin the setup

Miner + Balloon

2 cards
MinerBalloon

The chip card tanks the tower and distracts key defenders while the air win condition threatens a huge hit.

Best use cases

Use it when the opponent only has one reliable anti-air card or after they spend a building.

Pros and cons

  • Huge punish potential
  • Works well as surprise pressure
  • Fast anti-air cycle can shut it down

Evo Mortar + Skeleton Barrel + Cannon Cart

3 cards
Evo MortarSkeleton BarrelCannon Cart

The siege card demands a middle answer, aerial bait pressures the tower, and the ranged card punishes defenders.

Best use cases

Use it when the opponent overcommits to stopping the siege card or spends their small spell.

Pros and cons

  • Creates pressure in two spots
  • Baits small spells well
  • Can be tricky to pilot

Graveyard + Poison + Tornado + Ice Wizard

4 cards
GraveyardPoisonTornadoIce Wizard

The pull groups defenders, the slow effect weakens the counterpush, and the area spell turns control into tower damage.

Best use cases

Best after a defensive stop or when the opponent must defend inside an area spell.

Pros and cons

  • Excellent control package
  • Very strong into swarms
  • More expensive than it looks
