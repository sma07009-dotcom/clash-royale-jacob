Why this is bad

A troop being nearby is not a kill. It must acquire a legal target, wait through its hit timing, deal damage, and reduce that target's real health to zero.

Choose a counter, place it on your side, and keep enough elixir for the enemy you still need to defeat.

Win = every enemy dead in time + your spend is no higher than their spend.

Defeat the Wizard on a fair trade.

Kill the enemy troop before the clock reaches zero. The Defense budget is locked to the enemy's total card cost, so a 5-elixir Wizard can be answered with 5 or less. If the Wizard lands even one fireball on a Princess Tower, you lose immediately. After a clean defense, your surviving troops walk on toward the Princess Towers before the result appears.
