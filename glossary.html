Glossary

New-player termsSimple examplesGame language

Elixir

Elixir is the purple resource you spend to play cards. It fills slowly during the match, and every card has an elixir cost.

Example

If you have 10 elixir and play P.E.K.K.A. for 7, you only have 3 elixir left to defend or support it.

Win condition

A win condition is the main card your deck uses to damage the enemy tower.

Example

Hog Rider, Royal Giant, Balloon, Graveyard, X-Bow, Mortar, and Miner are common win conditions.

Cycle

Cycle means moving through your cards quickly so you can get back to an important card before your opponent reaches their answer.

Example

A Hog Rider deck can use Skeletons and Ice Spirit to cycle back to Hog Rider quickly.

Rotation

Rotation is the current order of cards in a player's hand and deck. Tracking it helps you know what answers are ready.

Example

If the opponent just used Cannon, their building may be out of rotation.

Positive elixir trade

A positive elixir trade happens when you spend less elixir defending than your opponent spent attacking.

Example

Using 1-elixir Skeletons to help stop a 4-elixir Mini P.E.K.K.A. is a strong trade.

Overcommit

Overcommitting means spending too much elixir on one play and leaving yourself weak to the next attack.

Example

Playing Balloon, Freeze, and Rage all at once can be an overcommit.

Punish

Punishing means attacking quickly because the opponent just spent a lot of elixir or used an important defensive card.

Example

If the opponent plays Golem in the back, attack the opposite lane before the push is ready.

Counterpush

A counterpush starts when your defensive cards survive and then become part of your attack.

Example

If Knight survives after defending, you can place Graveyard while Knight tanks.

Chip damage

Chip damage is small tower damage repeated over time instead of one huge push.

Example

Miner hits, Poison, Fireball, and small Goblin Barrel hits can all chip a tower down.

Splash damage

Splash damage hits multiple troops at once, making it useful against swarms and grouped support cards.

Example

Valkyrie, Baby Dragon, Wizard, Executioner, and Bomber can clear groups.

Spell bait

Spell bait means using multiple cards that tempt the same spell, then attacking once that spell is gone.

Example

Goblin Barrel and Goblin Gang both bait The Log.
