Bait

Good cards

Goblin BarrelSkeleton BarrelGoblin Gang

Bait decks force the opponent to use an important spell or counter, then punish once that answer is out of rotation.

How to play

Vary your placements and timing so the opponent cannot predict the same barrel, swarm, or pressure card every cycle.

Pros and cons

  • Very fast cycle speed
  • Forces predictable defensive responses
  • Needs spell tracking

Cycle

Good cards

Hog RiderIce SpiritSkeletons

Cycle decks use cheap cards to rotate back to one or two main win conditions faster than the opponent can reach their counters.

How to play

Defend with the lowest-cost answer that works, keep the pressure constant, and use repeated chip damage to win.

Pros and cons

  • Extremely fast pace
  • Great at out-rotating buildings
  • Small mistakes can snowball

Beatdown

Good cards

GolemNight WitchLightning

Beatdown decks build a huge push behind a tank, then use support troops and spells to clear the way to the tower.

How to play

Stay patient in single elixir and build your biggest pushes when you have enough elixir to support the tank.

Pros and cons

  • Beginner friendly structure
  • Very strong in double elixir
  • Can struggle against fast cycle pressure

Bridge Spam

Good cards

BanditRoyal GhostP.E.K.K.A

Bridge Spam decks pressure fast from the bridge with cards that demand quick, accurate answers.

How to play

Punish when the opponent spends too much elixir, then pressure before they can reset their defense.

Pros and cons

  • Easy to create pressure
  • Punishes defensive mistakes hard
  • Large tank matchups can be difficult

Control

Good cards

MinerPoisonFireball

Control decks slow the game down, defend efficiently, and win through repeated chip damage instead of one massive attack.

How to play

Stay calm, avoid overcommitting, and use spells and small pressure to control the pace of the match.

Pros and cons

  • Strong defensive versatility
  • Excellent for patient players
  • Very high skill barrier

Siege

Good cards

X-BowMortarTesla

Siege decks use a building as the win condition, then defend that building long enough for it to lock onto the tower.

How to play

Place the siege building when you can protect it, use defensive support, and rely on spells when needed.

Pros and cons

  • Forces immediate counterplay
  • Can pressure without crossing the bridge
  • Often requires defending at an elixir disadvantage
